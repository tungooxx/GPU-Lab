# v2.1 epistemic contract: before and after

## Before

- Result inspection, legacy repair, system work, and prospective science could
  all be represented as generic ResearchDecisions.
- `VERIFIED_REAL` could be read as execution reality and scientific evidence at
  the same time.
- A completed-looking outcome could update strategy aggregates without proving
  it came from a closed prospective scientific cycle.
- Rationale centered on transparent score rank.
- Meta-review counted all decisions together.

## After

- `decision_role`, `scientific_role`, `execution_verification`,
  `scientific_verification`, `cycle_status`, and `learning_namespace` separate
  operational and scientific meaning.
- Real GPU execution can be `SYSTEM_SMOKE` and `NOT_SCIENTIFIC` at once.
- Strategy eligibility fails closed unless the owning decision is a closed
  `SCIENTIFIC_ACTION` in `PRODUCTION_SCIENCE` with inspected outcome,
  hindsight, known information, and structured basis.
- Legacy/recovery/benchmark/system outcomes are excluded from strategy cohorts
  using the originating decision, not outcome payload guesses.
- `brain_step` persists central uncertainty and runner-up type/index, and
  explains its comparison rather than emitting score-only text.
- Meta-review separates scientific totals, closed cycles, administrative work,
  system verification, and strategy-eligible cycles.
